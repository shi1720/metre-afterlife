# Coding protocol

`radiations.csv`: all individually numbered frequency/wavelength assignments in CIPM 1983 Rec.1, 1992 Rec.3, 1997 Rec.1, except the twelve inherited lamp lines (separate file). 31 rows = 6 + 10 + 15. These are documentary assignments, not independent observations or instruments. Two methane subentries count separately. Nearby frequency offsets in 1997 section1.3, unenumerated hydrogen/rubidium/osmium transitions, alternative components allowed by the general clause, and relaxed iodine regimes in appendices are outside this bounded enumeration. A count is NOT total possible realizations.

`standard_relative` preserves the explicitly printed historical standard deviation (1983) or standard uncertainty (1992/1997). Do not divide rounded overall uncertainty by three in preference to an explicit value. `overall_relative` is kept separately. No independence is assumed across years or transitions. Frequency units are preserved and normalized in code. Wavelengths are vacuum wavelengths.

`key` is a longitudinal family identifier, NOT proof of equivalent operating conditions. The original section and transition label remain present. 576nm component o/a1 is not used for a cross-year uncertainty ratio; 1997 reclassifies this laser under section2.3. 633nm i/a13 is explicitly an alias; 612nm o/a7 and 515nm s/a3 likewise. Methane's 1992 split must not be collapsed to its best uncertainty.

The 1992 English HTML prints m for the unresolved methane wavelength. The official bilingual proceedings, printed p142 (PDF page160), gives fm; the data use that independently verified unit and retain a correction note. The 1983 English URL currently renders French; its numbers were checked against the proceedings and the English reproduction in Jennings et al.1987, pp13-14. Rows are transcribed and checked, not automatically OCR-parsed.

`legacy_lamps.csv`: the 12 explicit lines in 1997 section2.2. The 1983/1992 recommendations retain the 1963 lamp recommendations by reference. Do not present the 12 rows as newly enumerated in those editions or as independently observed operation.

`nrc_budget.csv`: NRC42753, printed p7, Table1 (PDF page8). Column contributions already include sensitivity coefficients. L is mm, contributions are nm. Entries combine in quadrature following the published calculation. They are not a newly measured uncertainty budget. Higher-order terms are retained exactly as supplied. Published summary coefficients12.4 and0.144 are rounded; summing displayed entries gives0.135048 rather than0.144 for the length coefficient. Both are preserved. The coarse0.1L alignment entry prevents attributing this difference to an error: unrounded source calculations are unavailable. It does not overturn dominance of non-laser contributions.
